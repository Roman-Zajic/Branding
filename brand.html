Phase 06
Voice & Tone
Four pillars. Plain copy rules.
Precision
Every number is deliberate. Clarity and accuracy — no approximations, no hand-waving.
Rigour
Assumptions are stated. Sources are cited. Confidence is earned, not asserted.
Transparency
We show our working. We explain the 'why' behind every decision.
Accessibility
Complex ideas deserve clear language. We translate without dumbing down.
DO Use specific, verified figures with currency and time period
DON'T Use vague amplifiers: "significant", "robust growth", "strong"
DO Name the model type and methodology
DON'T Make forward-looking statements without caveats
DO Use active voice: "the model projects £55.4m revenue"
DON'T Substitute a chart or design element for missing data
