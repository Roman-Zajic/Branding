Accordion
Expandable sections. Click a title to reveal or hide its content.
WACC is calculated using a blended cost of capital of 8.5%, reflecting current market risk premiums and a target capital structure of 60% equity / 40% debt. Terminal growth rate is set at 2.0%, consistent with long-run nominal GDP estimates. Projection horizon covers 10 fiscal years ending December 2034.
Revenue is modelled bottom-up across three segments: Enterprise (58% of total), SMB (29%), and Professional Services (13%). Growth rates are independently forecast per segment based on pipeline data, market sizing, and historic conversion rates.
Two-dimensional sensitivity tables are generated across WACC (7.0%–10.0%) and terminal growth (1.0%–3.5%) for implied equity value. At 8.5% WACC and 2.0% growth, central equity value is £148.2m. Bear case (10.0% / 1.0%) implies £104.6m; bull case (7.0% / 3.5%) implies £198.4m.

Numbered list
